📖 How to play
1
Look at the target number and the 4 numbers given.
2
Tap a number, an operator (+−×÷), then another number. The result becomes your new working number.
3
Chain as many operations as you need. Each original number can only be used once.
4
Reach exactly the target to win! If you go over or run out of numbers, the attempt fails.
e.g. target = 24, numbers = 3, 1, 2, 8
→ 3 + 1 = 4 → 4 × 2 = 8 → 8 × ... or 8 × 3 = 24 ✓
