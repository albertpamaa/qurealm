📖 How to play
1
Easy: 4 numbers (1-9), target 10-100. Hard: 8 numbers (1-15), target 150-500.
2
Tap a number, then an operator (+, −, ×, ÷), then another number to build your expression.
3
You can use each number only once. You don't have to use all of them.
4
When you reach the target, you win automatically. Use all your numbers without hitting the target and the attempt fails automatically.
5
Each attempt starts when you pick your first number. If you clear after starting, it counts as a failed attempt.
6
The 🔥 streak counts consecutive days you solve the puzzle. Miss a day and it resets to zero.
Example: target = 24, numbers = 3, 4, 6, 2
→ 6 × 4 = 24 ✓  or  3 × 4 = 12 → 12 + 6 = 18 → 18 + 6 = 24 ✓
