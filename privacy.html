Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Our commitment

QuRealm is designed with privacy by default. We do not collect personal data, require registration, or track individual users across sessions.

💾 What we store

Your game statistics (puzzles played, won, streak, last played date) are stored only in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Clearing your browser data will reset your stats.

Your language and difficulty preferences are also stored locally.

🍪 Cookies

QuRealm does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We may use anonymous analytics in the future (page views only). If we do, this policy will be updated.

🔗 Third parties

We use Google Fonts for typography. Google may log requests to its font servers according to Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party scripts are loaded.

✉️ Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at qrealmcontact@gmail.com
